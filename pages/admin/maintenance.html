Compact Report Snapshot History

Daily report-history snapshots (Branch/Officer/Location/Product) grow by one row per business day forever. This keeps only the last business day of each already-closed month (skipping weekends and Cambodian public holidays) and deletes the rest of that month's daily rows. The current month is never touched. This can't be undone.
