How these figures are calculated
  • Contribution = the row's NPL ÷ total NPL (absolute). NPL ratio = its NPL ÷ its own Loan Outstanding (relative) — a large portfolio can contribute most NPL with a low ratio.
  • Growth compares with the Daily History of the comparison date. New NPL comes from the NPL account history.
  • PAR — NBC Overdue, all classes ÷ Loan Outstanding.
  • PAR n = NBC Overdue value of accounts at least n days overdue ÷ Loan Outstanding (NBC OS). Totals are never averages of subgroup percentages.
  • Quadrants: high / low growth and risk against the thresholds shown above.
